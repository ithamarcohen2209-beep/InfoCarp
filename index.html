About InfoCarp

InfoCarp helps carp anglers and teams manage personal and group fishing sessions, shared competitions, catch reports, rod timers, sector and depth mapping, environmental conditions, rankings, statistics and Hall of Fame. Its Smart Fishing Journal turns recorded catches into live and historical insights about time, bait, distance, depth, weather and fishing patterns, with optional privacy-controlled community intelligence. Account access supports email and Google sign-in, and supported mobile platforms can receive native notifications.
