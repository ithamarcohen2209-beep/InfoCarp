What is deleted

Active personal data and shared personal copies are removed. Published competition/Hall archives can retain identifying data and permitted photos without automatic expiry. Private photo cleanup retries after temporary failures; recovery copies and exported files have separate retention as described below.

Actual retention and deletion

  • Account profiles, personal fishing logs, preferences, mappings and community contributions remain while the account is active. Account deletion removes associated active records and shared personal copies. Deleting a session alone retains historical catches.
  • Published competition results and Hall archives currently have no automatic expiry. They can retain angler and team names, weights, dates and permitted photos; they are not necessarily anonymous. Contact the operator to request review of personal information retained in these archives.
  • Private R2 photos not needed for permitted historical publication are deleted. After a successful account or group deletion, temporary cleanup or queue-confirmation failures are retried every 15 minutes, subject to infrastructure availability. Completion proof is recorded in the same database transaction; photos are not removed without valid deletion proof. Proof records contain a random identifier and timestamp, without user names or content.
  • The Supabase project currently uses Free, without the managed daily backups available on paid plans. D1 recovery history lasts 7 days on Free or 30 days on Paid; Workers logs last 3 or 7 days respectively. Deletion from active storage does not immediately remove these recovery copies. The deployed plan is checked operationally; this policy does not assert that additional external backups exist.
  • Offline copies are stored on the device. In-app account deletion clears the app's local cache on that device. Previously downloaded or exported files, external calendar copies and copies made by other group members cannot be remotely deleted.
