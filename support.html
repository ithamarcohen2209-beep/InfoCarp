Login codes

If an email verification code is incorrect, expired or does not arrive, use the Send a new code option on the verification screen. A short cooldown is applied before another code can be sent.

Smart Fishing Journal & community sharing

InfoCarp can calculate live and historical fishing insights from data you record, including catch time, weight, bait, distance, depth and available weather information. Saved sector/depth mappings are kept in a compact mapping library with an active mapping, search and sorting. Community sharing is optional and private by default. Sharing mode and field-level controls are available in Profile & Settings. Explicitly saving the current consent form can include eligible historical and future catches automatically; session and catch exclusions remain available. Anonymous Hall sharing hides names and photos. Changing to private removes automatic sharing, while manually published Hall catches are controlled separately.

Depth maps and calendar

Bathymetry uses saved measurements. Interpolated terrain appears only inside the measured area; sparse or collinear measurements can show a measured depth profile without a terrain surface. Competition invitations offer Google Calendar and ICS export. Saved calendar events must be updated manually if the schedule changes; native ICS sharing may require an updated mobile build.

Account & privacy

Account deletion can be initiated from within InfoCarp. If you no longer have access to the app, you can also request deletion using our public account deletion page. For privacy questions or assistance with your account, contact us using the support email above.
