Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026 · Operator: Team carp galilee

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use InfoCarp.

Information you provide

InfoCarp may process information you provide when creating or editing an account or profile, creating fishing sessions or competitions, recording catches, uploading photographs or ranking tables, entering sector measurements, and using other app features. This may include your name, email address or account identifier, profile information, competition and session data, catch data, photographs and notes.

Authentication

InfoCarp supports email-based authentication and optional Google sign-in. When you choose Google sign-in, the authentication provider may process account identifiers and profile information according to its own privacy terms. InfoCarp receives only the information required to authenticate and maintain your account.

Location and environmental data

If you grant location permission, InfoCarp may use device location to provide location-relevant weather information and associate environmental conditions with a catch or competition.

Camera, photos, microphone and speech

If you choose these features, InfoCarp may access the camera or selected photos to attach images to catch reports or ranking tables. Microphone and speech-recognition access may be used when you choose voice entry. Fish-photo relevance checks are designed to run locally in the app and do not require sending a catch photo to an external vision service solely for that validation.

AI-assisted processing

When an AI-assisted feature is used, such as processing a ranking table or interpreting user input, information required to perform that request may be transmitted to a third-party AI service provider. Do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information in content submitted for AI processing.

Weather and water-level services

InfoCarp may request weather and Kinneret water-level information from external data providers. Technical information necessary to perform these requests may be processed by those providers.

Optional community fishing intelligence

Community data sharing is off by default. A user may explicitly choose to contribute selected fishing data either anonymously or with credit. Even after enabling a community sharing mode, individual fishing sessions remain excluded until the user explicitly enables community sharing for that session. Users can stop sharing a session later, which removes that session's community contributions. Users can also control whether bait, distance ranges, depth ranges, weather information and a coarse location are included.

Anonymous contributions are designed to omit display names and precise GPS coordinates and to generalize measurements into ranges. InfoCarp may retain an internal account link solely to enforce the user's sharing choice, prevent duplicate contribution records, apply privacy thresholds and support later removal; this internal identifier is not shown as the contributor's identity in anonymous community outputs. Community insights are presented as aggregated statistics and may require data from multiple different anglers before a result is shown. If a user chooses credited sharing, their display name and/or team name may be associated with community contributions as described in the app.

Users can change the community-sharing mode back to private from Profile & Settings. Doing so removes their stored community contributions. Account deletion also removes community contribution records linked to that account.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, InfoCarp may register a device push token and notification preferences so the service can deliver competition, catch, invitation, reminder and other app notifications. Notification delivery may rely on platform services such as Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification service.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide and maintain InfoCarp, authenticate users, save and display competition and catch information, provide requested location and weather functionality, process user-requested AI features, improve reliability, prevent misuse and provide support.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary for app functions, such as hosting/database, authentication, weather/data, push notifications and AI services. Their processing is subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and account deletion

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service and meet legitimate legal, security and operational needs. Users may initiate account deletion using the in-app account deletion function. Users who no longer have access to the app can also request deletion through the public account deletion page. Associated user data will be deleted or de-identified unless retention is required for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or operational reasons.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

InfoCarp is not specifically directed to children. Applicable age and consent requirements should be followed.

Changes

We may update this policy as InfoCarp changes. The current version will be published on this page.
